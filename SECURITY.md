# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected security vulnerabilities affecting Tono products or services privately by emailing [contact@tonomemo.com](mailto:contact@tonomemo.com) with the subject **Security report**.

Include the affected product and version, a description of the potential impact, and reproduction steps when safe to share. Do not include passwords, access tokens, private notes, or unnecessary personal data.

Please do not disclose a suspected vulnerability publicly before coordinating with Tono. We will review the report and coordinate any follow-up by email.
